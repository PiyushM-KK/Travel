How the column gets written

  1. OneYou pick a directionA place, or just a month and a rough idea.
  2. TwoYou tell us the awkward partsDates, how many of you, who does not walk far, what the budget really is.
  3. ThreeWe send the column back filled inA day by day itinerary and a quotation, with hotel tiers priced separately.
  4. FourYou change itMost people do. That is the point of it being a column and not a brochure.
