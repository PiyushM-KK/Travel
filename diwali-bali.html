Is ₹1,15,000 the price per person?

Yes. It is the starting price per person for this package. The final price depends on your room sharing, dates and availability when you book. We confirm it in writing before you pay anything.

Will we actually be in Bali for Diwali?

Yes. Diwali falls on Sunday 8 November 2026, which is day 6 of this trip. You spend that evening at your private pool villa in Jimbaran.

Can I change the dates, hotels or departure city?

This is a FIT package, so it is planned just for your group. We can change hotels, add nights or look at other departure cities. Tell us in the form below and we will send a revised quote.

What isn't included?

Anything not on the "What's included" list above, such as visa fees, travel insurance, lunches and dinners, and personal expenses. Ask us and we will add what you need to your quote.

Send the enquiry form or message us on WhatsApp at +91 88660 50291. We don't take payments on this website; our team confirms everything with you directly.
