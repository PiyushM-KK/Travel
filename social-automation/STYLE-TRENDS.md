# Skyline AI image style - following what is trending (Gujarat / India)

Owner request 2026-10-28: "search what type of trend going in Gujarat India for AI images and create style to
follow that viral trend". Research the same day (web search; blogs and news, not measured rankings - treat as
indicators). Refresh this page monthly; a trend older than ~6 weeks is stale.

## What is trending (October 2026)

1. **Retro 80s photo look** - AI turns a modern photo into a 1980s film photograph (period colour, film grain, light
   leaks). Viral in India in September 2026; one widely shared version was "A Gujarat street in the 80s" (it then
   turned political - we take the LOOK only, never the politics).
2. **Festive glow** - Navratri / Garba edits (string lights, mirror-work colours) and now Diwali edits (rows of diyas,
   warm amber and gold light, rangoli, fairy lights). Gujarat's biggest festival season.
3. **"Tiny world" miniatures** - the figurine / miniature trend (Gemini "Nano Banana"), which took off in India first.

Not for Skyline: face edits of real people (needs each person's consent), AI deities ("Bhagwan Ji in car" - religious
sensitivity), political images, other brands' characters.

## The Skyline style (destinations, never people)

| Style | When | Prompt core |
|---|---|---|
| **Retro Postcard** (default for now) | any package | "Vintage 1980s Indian travel photograph, shot on faded Kodak film: <destination scene>. Soft film grain, slightly warm faded colours, gentle light leak in one corner, nostalgic retro postcard feel. No people, no text, no logos." |
| **Retro Diwali Glow** | Diwali window (until about 2 weeks after Diwali) | Retro Postcard + "a row of small clay diyas glowing along <a natural edge in the scene> and warm string lights, dreamy festive Diwali glow" |
| **Tiny World** | a carousel or a playful post, sparingly | "A miniature 3D diorama of <destination> on a small round base, tilt-shift, soft studio light, toy-like detail, no people, no text" |

lights on Kutchi bhunga huts - https://www.canva.com/M/MAHZUMWyeRs (media id MAHZUMWyeRs).

## Rules that do not change

- Every AI image is labelled "Illustrative - AI-generated" on the card and noted in the caption.
- A real Canva **Pro** photo, when used, is never reused (assets/canva-photos.json) and is not AI-restyled unless the
  owner chooses it (then it is labelled like an AI image).
- The scene must be TRUE to the destination (the Rann's white salt flat, Kutchi huts, Mizoram's hill towns): no
  invented landmarks, no wrong region.
- The price, package name and route on a card come only from Skyline's website catalogue (or the owner's own figure in
  the guided Offer card).
- Every post still waits for the owner's approve on WhatsApp.
